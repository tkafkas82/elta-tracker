PROmitheas Proactive Track & Trace

With PROmitheas Proactive Track & Trace, you don't need to look for your item, it will look for you! As soon as any of the tracking events you selected appears. You will receive a notification according to your preference. This is a free service and it is applicable for items with bar code XX123456789XX. Notification by SMS is provided only to Cypriot mobile numbers.

Important announcements:

For parcels originating from Germany, kindly note that the number of the form 999999999999 is an internal reference of Deutsche Post. In order to track the item , the barcode should be of the form CX123456789DE. You can obtain the correct form at the website https://nolp.dhl.de. You can enter the number and then it will be transfromed to the UPU standard CX123456789DE.
